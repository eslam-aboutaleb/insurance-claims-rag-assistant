# Example document for the ragkit minimal example.

## Greeter Policy

The greeter welcomes every visitor by name and offers
a cup of coffee on cold days. Visitors must sign in at
the front desk before entering the second floor.

## Refund Policy

Refunds are issued within 30 days of purchase with the
original receipt. Coffee refunds require the cup to be
at least half full. Refunds over $100 need a manager
signature.

## Office Hours

The office is open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. The
greeter desk closes at 4:30pm for the daily inventory.
